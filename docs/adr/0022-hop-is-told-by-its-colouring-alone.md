# Hop is told by its colouring alone

Supersedes the opacity half of ADR 0009. No paper fades by its hop: fill,
label and edge draw at full ink under every colouring, and a paper's hop
shows only under the Citation hop colouring (D10). The ramp 1, 0.9 … 0.4
left adjacent hops untellable, spent opacity, the channel emphasis and
search dim with, and under Citation hop said the hop twice. A second hop
channel (size, or a ring weight) was declined: size is kept for a future
metric, and a ring already means a paper not in Zotero (ADR 0020). Opacity
is left to emphasis and search.
