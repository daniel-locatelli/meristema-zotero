# A paper in Zotero fills; one not in Zotero is a ring

A library paper draws as a filled disc and an external paper as a ring in its
own colour, hollow on the plot's paper, under every colouring and in both
themes (F14, decided 2026-10-15). A hop paper matched to a library item is the
library's node and fills. The ring spans the disc's full radius, so hit-testing
is unchanged, and hop opacity, search and selection rings apply to it as to a
disc. The Key names the two kinds, In Zotero and Not in Zotero, only while the
plot shows both. This replaces the thin brightened ring a reached library
paper wore, which readers did not notice: one shape channel says it for every
paper, not just for hop results, and two outline meanings on one plot could
not be told apart.
