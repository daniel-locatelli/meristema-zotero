# One direction per graph

A seeded graph grows in one direction at a time, Citers or References, chosen
once for the whole graph in the rail; a graph never shows both, and to see the
references of a citer the reader makes the citer a seed. Walking both
directions from every seed doubles every hop and produces cloud-shaped graphs
that say nothing about lineage. The earlier per-seed `both` setting migrates
to Citers with a one-time notice (citation-hops spec, "State").

**Consequences**: graph state version 5 carries `direction`, and no rule,
colouring or count may assume a second direction exists.
