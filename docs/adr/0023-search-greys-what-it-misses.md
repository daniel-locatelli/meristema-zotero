# Search greys what it misses

The search box no longer hides papers or rings its matches (D36). A paper it
misses stays on the plot in the uniform colouring's grey at the Key's emphasis
alpha, with no new colour and no Key row. It takes no click, double-click, menu
or hover; its tooltip says "Clear search to select".

The search is a lens and changes nothing else: counts, the Key, colours,
regions, exports, Similar and the re-read all keep the whole scope, and a search
with no match greys everything rather than showing an empty graph. Filters are
what narrow a graph. Selecting from outside the plot (the rail, Zotero's list)
still works on a grey paper, since the reader asked for that paper by name.
